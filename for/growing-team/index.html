Where I help
Onboarding that doesn't scale

New hires read PDFs from 2021 and ask the same questions in Slack. Build a knowledge agent that answers from your actual current docs, with citations — so the answers are auditable and the docs stay live.

SharePoint is a graveyard

Years of policies, contracts, SOPs, and templates that nobody can find. A RAG-powered search agent surfaces the right document on the first try — and tells you when something's contradictory or outdated.

Sales follow-up is inconsistent

Some leads get four touches, some get one. Automate the sequence from first contact to qualified call without losing the human feel — and surface which reps actually need help versus which need pipeline.

Weekly reports take half a day

You're pulling numbers from four systems by hand on Friday morning. That's an automation problem masquerading as a reporting problem. Wire it once, run it on a schedule, your team gets Friday back.

You have Microsoft 365 and nobody knows what Copilot does

Before buying more licenses, find out which use cases actually return value at your company. A structured Copilot readiness assessment, no vendor pitch — an honest audit of where the ROI is, and where it isn't.

Support inbox floods on Monday

An AI triage layer routes, categorizes, and drafts responses for your team to approve — first-response time from six hours down to thirty minutes, without expanding the team. Humans still own every reply.

Power Automate flows that break every week

They were prototypes that became production. Now they fail silently and you find out from an angry client. Rebuild with proper error handling, logging, alerting, and version control — engineering, not duct tape.

AI that can't be trusted unsupervised

You want AI in the workflow but not making decisions or sending emails on its own. Design the oversight layer first — approval gates, audit logs, human-in-the-loop — so the automation is a tool, not a liability.

How an engagement looks
Discovery — 1 to 2 weeks

A focused review of the specific bottleneck. Output is a written assessment: what to build, what to buy, what to skip, in what order. Mapped to your existing stack and the budget you actually have.

Build — 4 to 8 weeks

Fixed-fee implementation. Wired into your existing Microsoft 365, Entra, SharePoint, or whatever stack you're on. Tested, documented, handed off to your team with the runbooks they need to operate it.

Advisory — monthly retainer

For teams that have engineers but want a second set of eyes on the agentic / AI side. Weekly check-ins, async PR review, on-demand for incidents. Optional, not a commitment.

Handoff is non-negotiable

Every engagement ships with IaC, runbooks, and architecture docs your team can operate without me. Nothing locked behind tribal knowledge. If your engineers can't pick it up, the work isn't done.
