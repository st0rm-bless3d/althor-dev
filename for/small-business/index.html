Where I help
Missed calls cost you jobs

You're losing work to competitors who answer the phone after hours. An AI agent takes messages, qualifies leads, books callbacks — and forwards anything urgent to your phone. Costs less per month than one missed booking.

Appointment confirmations eat your week

Your front desk spends two hours a day calling clients to confirm. Automate reminders, cancellations, and rescheduling — without firing anyone. Their time goes back to actual work.

Copy-paste between systems

Customer info from email into your CRM. Orders from Shopify into QuickBooks. Form submissions into a spreadsheet. That's three hours a week paying someone to do something a computer should handle in three seconds.

Proposals that take all afternoon

Your best proposals are a four-hour writing session you keep putting off. The next twenty should take twenty minutes — same quality, your voice, your pricing — with a custom assistant trained on what you've already written.

You bought Copilot. Nobody uses it.

Or Zapier. Or HubSpot AI. The tool isn't the problem — the configuration is. The structured rollout, the actual use cases, the prompts that fit your business. Fix the deployment instead of replacing the tool.

New clients wait three days to hear from you

Every new client requires the same twelve emails — intake form, welcome packet, scheduling, payment, kickoff. Automate the sequence so the relationship starts the day they sign, not when you remember.

Asking for reviews feels awkward

Your Google reviews are sporadic because the ask is uncomfortable. Build an automated request that goes out after every closed job, with a real link to the right place — not a generic "please review us."

Invoicing that should reconcile itself

Your bookkeeper spends a day a month matching payments to invoices that should already be linked. That's a solved problem — and freeing that day frees real budget.
