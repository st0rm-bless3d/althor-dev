## Claim rules

- Use the case studies for project descriptions. A service package is an offer, not evidence of a completed client engagement.
- The extraction throughput figure is self-reported and has no public measurement artifact. Omit it from profiles and proposals. If used in the case study, preserve that qualification.
- Omit audience counts, submission volumes, savings, and security approval claims unless evidence and permission support that use.
- Describe the work included. Do not promise security sign-off, a business result, or resolution within one session.
- Keep client identities private. Use only material approved for disclosure.
- Confirm availability and dependencies before quoting a date. Rates in these drafts preserve existing commercial choices; they are not market research.
